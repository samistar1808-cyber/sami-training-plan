Morning gym: getting going
  • Night beforeLay out gym clothes, pack your bag and fill your water bottle.
  • On wakingA big glass of water first thing. Coffee 30–60 min before you train if you want it.
  • ~7:45–8amSmall, light breakfast 60–90 min before: banana + Greek yoghurt, toast with honey or peanut butter, or a small bowl of oats.
  • No appetite?Even a banana or a slice of toast on the way is better than nothing.
  • AfterProtein + carbs within a couple of hours, like eggs on toast or a protein yoghurt bowl.
